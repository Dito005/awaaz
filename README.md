# Awaaz for Mac

Speak, and it writes. Select, and it reads.

Awaaz lives in your Mac's menu bar and works in Gmail, Outlook, Word, WhatsApp and any other app.

- **Dictate** (⌃⌥Space): talk in English, Urdu or both. Neat text is typed where your cursor is, translated if you like.
- **Read aloud** (⌃⌥R): select an email and hear it, in English or translated into Urdu.
- **Do it for me** (⌃⌥A): select an email and say “reply and tell him Tuesday at 3 works”. The reply is written for you.

## Download

**[Download Awaaz.dmg](https://github.com/Dito005/awaaz/releases/latest/download/Awaaz.dmg)** (macOS 13 Ventura or newer, Apple silicon and Intel)

## Install (2 minutes)

1. Open **Awaaz.dmg** and drag **Awaaz** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Awaaz from Applications. macOS says it can't verify the developer. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Awaaz. Enter your Mac password.
4. The welcome guide opens. Paste the two keys, allow the Microphone and Accessibility, and you're set.
   Awaaz starts by itself every time you log in.

## Keys and cost

Awaaz uses your own AI accounts. Keys stay in your Mac's Keychain.

| Job | Service | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|
| Voice to text | [Groq](https://console.groq.com/keys) | about $0.002 per minute spoken |
| Polish and translate | [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys) | a fraction of a cent per message |
| Read aloud | Mac voice (free), OpenAI or ElevenLabs (optional) | free to a few cents |

Around 20 emails a month usually costs well under $1.
