# Awaaz for Mac

Speak, and it writes. Open an email, and it reads it to you, in English or Urdu.

- **Speak button** at the bottom of your screen: click, talk in Urdu, English or both, click ✓. Your words appear in a card. Tap **Polish** for a neat version, pick a language, then **Paste**.
- **Inside Gmail** (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc): **Read aloud** with an English / اردو picker, **Reply by voice**, and a **Speak** button in every reply box.
- Works everywhere else too: Outlook, Word, WhatsApp, Safari. Shortcut ⌃⌥Space.
- Nothing to sign up for and no keys to enter. It just works.

## Download

**[Download Awaaz.dmg](https://github.com/Dito005/awaaz/releases/latest/download/Awaaz.dmg)** (macOS 13 Ventura or newer, Apple silicon and Intel)

## Install

1. Open **Awaaz.dmg** and drag **Awaaz** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Awaaz from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check the developer: click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Awaaz walks you through the rest with big buttons: allow the microphone, drag the Awaaz icon into the Accessibility list, and click **OK** to add the Gmail buttons.

Awaaz starts by itself every time you turn on your Mac.
